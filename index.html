05
Strategic Context

The Footprint Story Behind the Deal

The Webster acquisition should be viewed against a decade of branch network reshaping at both companies. Santander's U.S. branch network is materially smaller today than it was during the Sovereign Bank era, when Santander described a network of more than 670 branches and nearly 2,000 ATMs. Webster, meanwhile, reduced its banking center count through 2021 before expanding meaningfully through the Sterling merger in 2022. The result is a transaction that is not just about assets, deposits, and EPS accretion. It is also about rebuilding Northeast density after years of branch rationalization on both sides.18

Santander

The large Sovereign-era branch footprint has been materially reduced over the past decade. Santander entered the period with more than 600 domestic offices and rationalized that network significantly through branch closures and consolidations.

Webster

Webster pruned its banking center network through 2021, then expanded meaningfully through the Sterling Bancorp merger in 2022. That transaction added New York metro density, commercial banking scale, and new geographic reach.

The deal thesis

Santander is not simply buying Webster to get larger. It is buying a reshaped Northeast branch, deposit, and commercial banking platform after years of network rationalization, using Webster to rebuild physical density in the region.

06
Corporate Actions Mechanics

How the ADS Consideration Gets Delivered

The stock portion of the consideration is the operationally interesting part. It is a fixed exchange ratio rather than a fixed dollar value, and it is paid in newly issued depositary shares.

Each Webster share converts into 2.0548 Santander American Depositary Shares, and each ADS represents one Santander ordinary share.15 Citibank N.A. has acted as depositary for Santander's ADR program since 2021, replacing J.P. Morgan in that role.56

To deliver the stock leg, Santander issues additional ordinary shares. Those shares are deposited and represented by newly issued ADSs distributed to former Webster holders. The issuance of new ordinary shares and corresponding ADSs is a stated dilution factor in the deal disclosures.1

Because 2.0548 is not a whole number, most positions produce fractional ADS entitlements. Under standard practice, the exchange agent aggregates the fractions, sells them in the market, and distributes cash in lieu of fractional ADSs. The Form F-4 registration statement and the proxy statement and prospectus govern the exact treatment.1

07
Corporate Actions: The Full Sequence

ADS Delivery and the Post-Closing Exchange Window

The stock consideration does not end with the initial delivery of Santander ADSs. The proxy statement notes that Webster holders may exchange their Santander ADSs for Santander ordinary shares at no charge during a specified period following closing, subject to applicable procedures and certifications. That post-closing election introduces a second processing path that most deal summaries omit entirely.10

01
Webster Common Share

A holder of Webster Financial shares immediately prior to the effective time of the merger. Starting point for the full consideration-processing sequence.

02
Reincorporation Merger into Webster Virginia

Webster Financial Corporation merges into a newly formed Virginia entity. Each Webster share converts into one Webster Virginia share. This step redomiciles the company under Virginia law to enable the share exchange that follows.

03
Statutory Share Exchange

Immediately following the reincorporation, Santander acquires all Webster Virginia shares in a statutory share exchange under Virginia law. Webster becomes a wholly owned Santander subsidiary.

04
$48.75 Cash + 2.0548 Santander ADSs Delivered

Each Webster share converts into $48.75 in cash and 2.0548 newly issued Santander American Depositary Shares, each representing one Santander ordinary share listed on the NYSE. The ADS ratio is fixed; the dollar value of the stock leg floats with Santander pricing from signing through closing, an explicitly noted risk factor in the proxy.1

05
Fractional ADS Entitlements: Aggregated, Sold, Cash In Lieu

Because 2.0548 is not a whole number, virtually every position produces a fractional ADS entitlement. The exchange agent aggregates all fractional interests, sells them in the open market, and distributes the net proceeds pro rata to entitled former Webster holders. Santander bears the cost of those sales. No fractional Santander ADSs are issued.10

06
Post-Closing ADS-to-Ordinary-Share Exchange Window

Following closing, Santander ADS holders may exchange their ADSs for Santander ordinary shares at no charge during a specified period, subject to procedures and certifications set out in the letter of transmittal. This optional step adds a second, post-event processing path: holders seeking direct ownership of the underlying Spanish-listed shares rather than the NYSE depositary receipt structure must navigate a separate election process. Absent any election, former Webster holders remain Santander ADS holders. Beneficial owners will follow the exchange procedures communicated by their brokers, custodians, or nominees.10

Corporate Actions Operational Note

This is not a straightforward cash-and-stock merger. For custodians, brokers, and asset servicers, the event requires separate processing across four tracks: cash consideration delivery; ADS share consideration; cash-in-lieu for fractional ADS entitlements (cost borne by Santander); and the optional post-closing ADS-to-ordinary-share election process. Former Webster holders finish the transaction as holders of a Spanish bank ADS structure, not U.S. bank common stock, unless they subsequently elect to convert. The letter of transmittal contains the exchange instructions. Beneficial owners processing through intermediaries will need to follow the procedures their brokers or custodians communicate.

Path to Close

Deal Timeline

From the pricing reference date through the expected second-half 2026 close. Completion remains subject to bank regulatory approvals in the United States and the European Union and to the approval of both companies' stockholders.1

February 2, 2026

Pricing Reference Date

Santander's closing price on this date sets the $75.59 implied per-share value and the aggregate of approximately $12.3 billion.1

February 3, 2026

Definitive Agreement Announced

Webster and Santander announce a signed definitive agreement, unanimously approved by Webster's board and the relevant Santander bodies, under which Webster becomes a wholly owned subsidiary of Santander. The deal is structured in two steps, a reincorporation merger followed by a statutory share exchange in which each Webster share converts into the cash and ADS consideration.110

March 30, 2026

Bank-Level Merger Agreement and OCC Filing

Santander Holdings USA, Santander Bank N.A., and Webster Bank N.A. execute a bank-level Agreement and Plan of Merger, and a Bank Merger Act application is submitted to the OCC. The bank merger is conditioned on the closing of the holding company transactions and OCC approval.7

May 26, 2026

Webster Stockholders Approve

At a special meeting, Webster stockholders approved the transaction with 115,788,667 shares in favor and 1,279,203 against, with 72.3 percent of eligible shares represented. A separate advisory vote on deal-related executive compensation also passed, by a narrower margin. Regulatory approvals and other customary conditions remain outstanding.89

Second Half 2026

Expected Close

Closing is expected in the second half of 2026, subject to remaining regulatory approvals, including those of the Federal Reserve Board and the European Central Bank. Both companies' stockholder approvals have been obtained. Closing remains targeted for the second half of 2026.1
